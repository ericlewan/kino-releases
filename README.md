# Kino

Kino for macOS: browse, stream and download movies and shows, and play them on your AirPlay TV.

## Install

1. Download the `.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ericlewan/kino-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag **Kino** into **Applications**.
3. Open **Kino** from Applications. The first time, macOS stops it with **“Kino” Not Opened** (“Apple could not
   verify…”). That's expected: Kino isn't from the App Store. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll to the bottom. Next to *“Kino” was blocked to protect your
   Mac*, click **Open Anyway**, enter your Mac's password, then click **Open Anyway** once more.

You only need to do this once; updates open normally. If the Open Anyway button isn't there, open Kino again
first: it only shows for about an hour after macOS stops the app.

Needs macOS 14 or later.

## Updates

Kino checks for updates once a day and offers to install them. You can also check now from
**Kino → Check for Updates…**.
